Executive Summary

This documentation details the complete architectural, algorithmic, and software blueprint for the Smart Food-Waste Reduction Framework tailored specifically to commercial Quick Service Restaurants (QSRs) operating across the South-East geo-political zone of Nigeria.

The system targets prominent chain brands including Crunchies Fried Chicken, Kilimanjaro Restaurant, and Chicken Republic across seven major South-East commercial centers: Enugu, Awka, Owerri, Abakaliki, Umuahia, Onitsha, and Aba.

Core Innovation

Eliminating kitchen overproduction by marrying pre-trained Time-Series Foundation Models (Amazon Chronos-Bolt & Google TimesFM) with a South-East Nigerian Local Feature Layer (Igbo market days, civil service paydays, and church surges) into a staggered prep batching engine.

3. AI Demand Forecasting Engine

Instead of training data-hungry neural networks from scratch, the system integrates two state-of-the-art Time-Series Foundation Models (TSFMs) in a dual-model weighted ensemble:

⚡ Amazon Chronos-Bolt

Tokenized language-model approach (T5). Excels at tracking erratic localized spikes, sudden volume swings, and daily non-linear fluctuations.

🌐 Google TimesFM

Decoder-only patch transformer trained on 100B+ points. Delivers remarkable baseline stability, seasonal rhythms, and robust trend extrapolation.

Operational Rule: Disagreement-Based Staggered Batching

• High Agreement (Difference < 8%): High confidence. Kitchen cooks the full quota in standard opening batches.
• High Disagreement (Difference ≥ 8%): High volatility detected. The system automatically enforces a 2-stage prep: cook 65% for the lunch rush, and prepare the remaining 35% on-demand during the afternoon. This stops overproduction waste.

4. South-East Nigeria Local Feature Layer

Global foundation models are calibrated with four essential regional operational drivers:

🏛️ Igbo Market Day Cycles

4-day rotation (Eke, Oye, Afor, Nkwo) causing foot-traffic surges in major commercial hubs like Onitsha Main Market and Aba.

💳 Salary / Payday Windows

Pronounced dining surges during the 25th–31st of each month in administrative civil-service capitals (Enugu, Abakaliki, Awka).

⛪ Sunday Church Surges

Substantial lunch rushes between 12:30 PM and 3:30 PM on Sundays, alongside Saturday wedding catering peaks.

🌧️ Rainy Season Disruptions

Afternoon downpours that depress walk-in customer flow while triggering sudden spikes in deliveries and takeout.

5. Database Schema Blueprint

The backend is powered by 13 relational tables mapping directly to the study's research objectives:

Table Name Cluster Purpose & Key Fields
RestaurantChain Organization Parent brand name, headquarters city (Crunchies, Kilimanjaro, etc.)
Branch Organization Name, city, state, standby generator flag, is_pilot_intervention (Treatment vs Control)
UserProfile Accounts User link, branch assignment, system roles (Cook, Storekeeper, Manager, Auditor)
Ingredient Menu/Inventory Raw item name, unit of measure, unit cost in Naira (₦), chilled and ambient shelf-life
MenuItem Menu Finished dish name, category (Rice, Protein, Sides), selling price in ₦
RecipeBOM Menu Bill of Materials: maps dish portions to raw ingredient quantities with shrinkage %
InventoryBatch Inventory Stock batch received, quantity remaining, expiry date (FEFO rotation order)
PowerOutageLog Inventory Records duration of electrical power/generator outages affecting refrigeration
LocalCalendarContext Calendar Date, city, traditional market day, payday window flag, weather condition
SalesRecord Sales POS transactions: branch, dish, sale date, shift, portions sold, gross revenue (₦)
DailyForecastItem Forecasting Chronos prediction, TimesFM prediction, ensemble forecast, batch recommendation
KitchenPrepTarget Forecasting Calculated raw ingredients (kg) to prepare per shift, actual used, variance
WasteLog Waste Disposal stage, menu item or raw ingredient, weight (kg), auto-calculated ₦ loss, root cause
MenuEngineeringSnapshot Analytics Dishes classified into Star, Plowhorse, Puzzle, Dog with operational recommendations

6. Interactive Swagger UI API Specification

All endpoints are documented with OpenAPI 3.0 via drf-spectacular and testable at /api/docs/:

Method Endpoint Functional Action
POST /api/v1/auth/login/ Authenticate staff credentials and issue API token
GET /api/v1/organization/branches/ List all QSR branches with city and pilot intervention tags
GET /api/v1/menu/items/ Retrieve dishes with retail prices and Recipe BOM linkages
GET /api/v1/calendar/today/?city=ONITSHA Get today's Igbo market day, payday window, and weather
POST /api/v1/waste/logs/ Log waste event (kg, stage, cause) with instant ₦ loss valuation
GET /api/v1/waste/daily-summary/ Fetch shift waste totals and stage breakdown in Naira
POST /api/v1/forecast/trigger/ Execute Chronos + TimesFM ensemble & generate kitchen prep targets
GET /api/v1/forecast/prep-sheet/ Serve tomorrow's digital kitchen prep sheet to cooks
GET /api/v1/analytics/menu-engineering/ Retrieve Star/Plowhorse/Puzzle/Dog menu profitability matrix
GET /api/v1/analytics/pilot-comparison/ Compare Treatment vs Control branches for Phase 5 evaluation
